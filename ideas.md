## Leash system

A new system where there are leash items that aren't weapons but can attach to existing ones and have unique effects.  
e.g: "Wand of sparking" + "Hipotetical leash item" = Fire leash.

The system consists in:
Keep a leash item with unique personal buff (+defense to owner, crit chance and movement, etc.) inside your inventory, when right clicking a puppy with a leashable weapon (e.g. wand of sparking) a weapon-unique leash is release from the owner to the puppy giving puppy a unique buff.
