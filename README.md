v1.5.13 - for Terraria 1.4.4+

Puppy Mod is a small puppy-focused mod I made for myself. I always liked the vanilla Dog Ears and Dog Tail, so I wanted them to actually feel like being a puppy - cute, a bit useful, and totally optional.

All barks and woofs were recorded by me, so when you bark it really is me. :3

### What it does

- **Become a puppy -** Equip both Dog Ears + Dog Tail to feel instantly more puppy! You get the set free on a new character if you want it in configs, and you can take them off anytime to be you again. *arf!*

- **Mix and match -** Dog Ears helps with digging, Dog Tail gives you zoomies for moving and jumping. Both work in vanity too, just softer. Equip both for **Puppy bonus: Double tap DOWN to bark, arf!** - even in vanity!

- **Get praised! -** An owner with a Clicker can praise every nearby puppy at once, giving them the Good Puppy buff - you feel happier and zoomier for a bit. Puppies can't click - they get to be good. *clack!*

- **Bark for attention -** Double-tap DOWN to bark whenever you want - it sounds higher and happier right after you've been praised! *awoo!*

- **Walkies -** If you want, wear a collar. An owner with a leash or leash weapon can right-click you to clip on - you go for walkies together and you both get a buff while tethered. Right-click again to let go. *wag wag!*

Everything is opt-in. Be a happy solo pupper or bring friends - it's fun either way.

### Good to know - woof?

**Solo or with friends?** Both work! Be a happy solo pupper, or bring friends to share the fun!

**Two puppies?** Yep - the whole pack can be good together!

**Can puppies praise themselves?** Nuh-uh - only owners praise, puppies get to be good. That's the fun! :3

### Configs

There are client and server configs if you want to tweak it - like whether new characters start with the Dog Ears and Tail, and a few client preferences. No need to touch them if you just want to play.

### Install

Terraria 1.4.4+ with tModLoader, enable the mod and reload. That's it. Works single and multiplayer, no dependencies.
