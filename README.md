## What is this mod?

Puppy Mod adds a soft, teasing owner / puppy dynamic to Terraria — wholesome, affectionate, a little kinky, but **never explicit**. Think collar jingles, tail wags, zoomies around the arena, and that warm feeling when someone says "good puppy!"

Built for couples, friends, servers, and anyone who wants to be a little more puppy while digging, fighting, and exploring. Furry and therian friendly. Consensual — you choose when to put the ears on and when to take them off.

No NSFW content. Just praise, zoomies, and barks. And yes — **every single bark, woof, growl and clicker sound you hear is literally me :D** I recorded them myself, so when you get praised you're hearing *your* puppy. Arf!

## How to Play

### 1. Become Puppy

Equip **Dog Ears** on your head (vanity, slot 10) and **Dog Tail** as an accessory. The moment both are on, you let out a little bark — you've transformed for the first time.

You get both items free when you make a new character. No grinding, just puppy.

Take either piece off and you're you again. Totally your choice.

### 2. Choose Your Form

How you wear the tail decides what kind of puppy you are:

**Furry — Tail as vanity (slots 13+)**
You wear the tail just for looks, so you keep all your real accessory slots free. You feel a little faster, a little bouncier — casual and cozy, perfect for wandering, building, and being cute without giving anything up.

**Therian — Tail as a real accessory (slots 3-9)**
You wear the tail for real, giving up an accessory slot to commit. You feel it right away — much faster, springy jumps, you can really run. Built for zoomies, boss arenas, and showing your Owner how hard you can go.

You can swap anytime. Wear it as vanity for the journey, clip it on properly when it's time to be serious puppy. It changes instantly and shows an armor set bonus.

### 3. Get Clicked

This is the heart of it. A friend who *isn't* a puppy — your Owner — holds **The Clicker** (that defines the praise range) and uses it while standing close to you (that sends the signal).

- You hear the sharp, satisfying click — range is locked at the moment of the click.
- You get the **Good Puppy!** glow — the screen tells you to look how good you are, and you feel it.
- You bark, and you sound happier — a little higher, a little brighter because you know you did well.

One click is enough to praise every good puppy nearby, so if there's a whole pack, everyone gets loved at once. The buff refreshes as long as you stay in range and your Owner keeps clicking.

And if you try to click yourself while you're still a puppy? You get a soft growl instead. Only someone else can tell you you're a good puppy — that's the point. :3

### 4. Zoomies & Bark!

That Good Puppy! feeling makes everything better:

- You heal a little faster, like you're being looked after
- You run noticeably faster, that classic puppy burst of energy
- You jump higher and lighter, ready to bounce around the arena

And every bark is a little different — pitch variance keeps it alive, and it goes **higher when you're happy** (when you have Good Puppy!).

**New!** As a furry or therian (ears + tail), **double-tap UP to bark on command** — armor set bonus. Show your Owner you want attention. Spam-protected so you can't be *too* noisy.

## FAQ

**Is this NSFW? Will I get in trouble on Steam?**
No. 100% SFW and Steam-safe. Wholesome with a playful, teasing edge. Think headpats and praise, not explicit content.

**Can I play solo?**
Absolutely. You can still be a puppy, enjoy feeling quicker and springier, and bark with double-tap UP. You just need another player nearby to get that Good Puppy! click.

**What if two puppies are near an Owner?**
Both hear it. One thoughtful click praises the whole pack nearby (if they were in range *when* it clicked).

**Can puppies click other puppies?**
No — if you're already wearing ears and tail, you're a puppy, and the Clicker will just growl at you. Take the gear off to be an Owner again.

**Does the Good Puppy! feeling stack?**
It refreshes. Stay close while your Owner clicks and that warm zoomy feeling just keeps going. It wants you to stay near.

**Why does my bark sound higher sometimes?**
Because you're happy. When you have Good Puppy! you bark brighter and higher. Every bark has a little randomness too, so it always feels alive.

**How do I bark on command?**
Be a puppy (ears + tail) and **double-tap UP** — you'll do a happy bark! It shows as your armor set bonus.

**What about that leash I saw mentioned?**
Soon. A good puppy might need a walk in a future update.

## Installation

1. Install **tModLoader 1.4.4+** on Steam
2. Subscribe on the Workshop or drop the `.tmod` file into `Documents/My Games/Terraria/tModLoader/Mods`
3. Enable Puppy Mod in the Mods menu and Reload
4. Make a new character (or use an existing one — check the Guide for the Clicker recipe at a Work Bench)
5. Put on Dog Ears and Dog Tail, find an Owner, get clicked, enjoy the zoomies — double-tap UP to arf!

No dependencies. Works in single-player and multiplayer. Crafting is discoverable via the Guide at a Work Bench.
