> A playful, consensual, 100% SFW pet-play mod for Terraria. Put on the ears, wag your tail, and wait for the click that tells you how good you are.

## What is this mod?

I made Puppy Mod to add a soft, teasing owner/puppy dynamic to Terraria - wholesome, affectionate, a little kinky, but never explicit. Think collar jingles, tail wags, zoomies around the arena, and that warm feeling when someone says "good puppy!"

I built it for couples, friends, servers, and anyone who wants to be a little more puppy while digging, fighting, and exploring. Furry and therian friendly. Consensual - you choose when to put the ears on and when to take them off.

No NSFW content. Just praise, zoomies, and barks. All barks, woofs and clicker sounds were recorded by me, so when you get praised you are hearing me.

## How to Play

### 1. Become Puppy

Equip Dog Ears on your head (vanity) and Dog Tail as an accessory. The moment both are on, you let out a little bark - you have transformed for the first time.

You get both items free when you make a new character. No grinding, just puppy.

Take either piece off and you are you again. Totally your choice.

### 2. Choose Your Form

How you wear the tail decides what kind of puppy you are:

**Furry - Tail as vanity**  
You keep all functional accessory slots. You feel a little faster and bouncier - casual and cozy for wandering and building.

**Therian - Tail as a real accessory**  
You give up a slot to commit. Much faster, higher jumps, built for zoomies and boss arenas.

You can swap anytime. It changes instantly and shows an armor set bonus.

### 3. Get Clicked

A friend who is not a puppy - your Owner - holds The Clicker to set the praise range, then uses it to send a signal. Every puppy in range at that moment gets the Good Puppy buff.

- You hear the click.
- You get the Good Puppy glow and feel the effects.
- Your barks sound happier and a little higher.

One click praises every puppy nearby. The buff refreshes while you stay in range and your Owner keeps clicking. Puppies cannot use the clicker themselves. That is the point. Puppies get clicked.

### 4. Zoomies and Bark

While you have Good Puppy you heal faster, run faster, and jump higher. Every bark has pitch variation, and it goes higher when you are happy.

As a furry or therian you can double tap UP to bark on command. It is your armor set bonus.

## FAQ

**Is this NSFW? Will I get in trouble on Steam?**
No. 100% SFW and Steam safe. Wholesome with a playful, teasing edge.

**Can I play solo?**
Yes. Enjoy the bonuses and bark with double tap UP. You need another player nearby to receive a click.

**What if two puppies are near an Owner?**
Both are praised. One click affects every puppy in range at that moment.

**Can puppies click each other?**
No. Puppies cannot use the clicker. Only non-puppies can.

**Does Good Puppy stack?**
It refreshes. Stay close and it keeps going.

**Why does my bark sound higher sometimes?**
Because you are happy. With Good Puppy you bark brighter and higher.

**How do I bark on command?**
Be a puppy and double tap UP.

## Installation

1. Install Terraria 1.4.4+ on Steam
2. Subscribe on Workshop or drop the .tmod into Documents/My Games/Terraria/tModLoader/Mods
3. Enable Puppy Mod in the Mods menu and Reload
4. Make a new character (or use an existing one - check the Guide for the Clicker at a Work Bench)
5. Put on Dog Ears and Dog Tail, find an Owner, get clicked, enjoy the zoomies

No dependencies. Works in single player and multiplayer.
