[h1]What is Puppy Mod?[/h1]
Puppy Mod adds a soft, teasing [b]owner / puppy[/b] dynamic to Terraria — wholesome, affectionate, a little kinky, but [b]never explicit[/b]. Think headpats, praise, zoomies around the boss arena, and happy barks when someone says [i]"good puppy!"[/i]

Built for couples, friends, servers, and anyone who wants to be a little more puppy while digging, fighting, and exploring. [b]Furry and therian friendly.[/b] Consensual — you choose when to put the ears on and when to take them off. No forced effects, no NSFW content. Just good puppy energy.

[b]SFW and Steam-safe:[/b] Flirty and teasing, but completely wholesome. Safe for any server.

[h1]How to Play[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]1. Become Puppy[/b] — Equip [b]Dog Ears[/b] (vanity, slot 10) + [b]Dog Tail[/b] (vanity or accessory). The instant both are on you [b]bark[/b] automatically — that's literally me! You get both items free on character creation.
[*][b]2. Choose Your Form[/b] — Where you put your tail matters. [b]Furry[/b] (vanity) = cozy, keep all slots. [b]Therian[/b] (functional) = sacrifice a slot to be much faster and bouncier. Swap anytime, shows as set bonus.
[*][b]3. Get Clicked[/b] — A non-puppy Owner [b]holds[/b] The Clicker to set range, then [b]clicks[/b] to send the signal. Every puppy in range [b]at that moment[/b] gets the [b]Good Puppy![/b] buff and barks happier and higher.
[*][b]4. Zoomies & Bark![/b] — Buff gives regen and zoomies, refreshed while you stay close. And as a puppy you can [b]double-tap UP to bark on command[/b] whenever you want attention!
[/list]

[h1]Furry vs Therian — Choose Your Devotion[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Furry[/b] — Tail in vanity slots (13+). Light and cozy, keep all functional slots. Nice for building and exploring.
[*][b]Therian[/b] — Tail in functional slots (3-9, including extra slots). Much faster and springier. Full commitment. Your Owner will have to chase you.
[/list]
Swapping slots instantly changes form — vanity for travel, functional for serious puppy time.

[h1]The Clicker[/h1]
Pink rarity. Hold it to set your praise range, click to send a crisp, satisfying click. Puppies who try to click themselves get a soft growl instead — only an Owner can tell you you're good. That's the point. :3
Craft it at a [b]Work Bench[/b] — check the [b]Guide[/b] for the recipe.

Remember: only humans (no ears/tail) can be Owners.

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[b]Is this NSFW?[/b] No. 100% SFW, wholesome with a teasing edge. Steam-safe and server-friendly.

[b]Can I play solo?[/b] Yes! Be a puppy, enjoy the bonuses and bark with double-tap UP, or invite someone to click you.

[b]Two puppies near one Owner?[/b] Both get praised. One click gives every good puppy in range at click time the Good Puppy buff.

[b]Can puppies click each other?[/b] No — puppies who try get a growl. Only non-puppies can click.

[b]How do I bark?[/b] Automatically when you become a puppy or get praised, plus [b]double-tap UP[/b] anytime you're furry/therian.

[b]What about leash.png?[/b] A good puppy might need a walk soon. Sit, stay.
