[h1]what does it do? ฅ•ω•ฅ[/h1]
Turn your adventure into walkies! This mod lets you be a little more puppy while you dig, fight and explore — with playful praise and cozy owner/puppy moments with friends. It's light, consensual, and you decide when to be puppy and when not. [i]Gentle and SFW — just headpats and happy zoomies![/i]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]how to be puppy — arf![/h1]
[list]
[*][b]Become a puppy —[/b] Equip both [b]Dog Ears + Dog Tail[/b] to feel instantly more puppy! You get the set free on a new character, and you can take them off anytime to be you again. *arf!*
[*][b]Choose your style —[/b] Wear the Tail in [b]vanity[/b] for cozy [b]Furry[/b] mode (+ movement speed and mining speed) or in an [b]accessory slot[/b] for bouncy [b]Therian[/b] mode (much faster running and big jumps). The set bonus tells you what you picked!
[*][b]Get praised! —[/b] A non-puppy holding the [b]Clicker[/b] can praise every nearby puppy at once, giving them the [b]Good Puppy![/b] buff for health regen and happy zoomies! Puppies can't click — they get to be good. *clack!*
[*][b]Bark for attention —[/b] Double-tap [b]UP[/b] to bark whenever you want — it sounds higher and happier right after you've been praised! *awoo!*
[*][b]Walkies —[/b] The puppy must wear the [b]Chain Leash[/b]; any owner can then [b]right-click[/b] the puppy (even empty-handed) to clip on a soft brown rope that keeps you close. While tethered you get a continuous happy effect with a gentle tug to stay nearby — right-click again to unclip and toggle it off! *wag wag!*
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]good to know — woof?[/h1]
[b]Solo or with friends?[/b] Both work! Be a happy solo pupper, or bring friends to share the fun!
[b]Two puppies?[/b] Yep — the whole pack can be good together!
[b]Can puppies praise themselves?[/b] Nuh-uh — only owners praise, puppies get to be good. That's the fun! :3
