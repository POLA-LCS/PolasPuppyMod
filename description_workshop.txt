[h1]Cute features[/h1]
[b]Become a puppy![/b] Equip both [b]Dog Ears + Dog Tail[/b] to feel instantly more puppy! You get the set free on a new character if you want it in configs, and you can take them off anytime to be you again. *arf!*

[b]Mix and match![/b] [b]Dog Ears[/b] helps with digging, [b]Dog Tail[/b] gives you zoomies for moving and jumping (both softer in vanity). Equip both for [b]Puppy bonus: Double tap DOWN to bark, arf![/b] even in vanity!

[b]Get praised![/b] A non-puppy holding the [b]Clicker[/b] can praise every nearby puppy at once, giving them the [b]Good Puppy![/b] buff for happy zoomies! Puppies can't click, they get to be good. *clack!*

[b]Bark for attention![/b] Double-tap [b]DOWN[/b] to bark whenever you want, and it sounds higher and happier right after you've been praised! *awoo!*

[b]Walkies![/b] If you want, wear a [b]collar[/b]. An owner with a [b]leash or leash weapon[/b] can right-click you to clip on, and you both get a buff while tethered. Right-click again to let go. *wag wag!*

[hr][/hr]
[h1]become a real puppy - awoo![/h1]
With the full set equipped and [b]no mount[/b], press your [b]Quick Mount[/b] key (default [b]R[/b]) to truly transform into a dog! Press again to be you. If you have a mount equipped, the key mounts instead and you happily ride off.

While you're a real puppy items are off, and you stay a puppy in tight spots until there's room to stand back up. *tail wag!*

[hr][/hr]
[h1]good to know - woof?[/h1]
[b]Solo or with friends?[/b] Both work! Be a happy solo pupper, or bring friends to share the fun!

[b]Two puppies?[/b] Yep - the whole pack can be good together!

[b]Can puppies praise themselves?[/b] Nuh-uh - only owners praise, puppies get to be good. That's the fun! :3

[b]Technical woofs![/b] Needs the [b]MorphAPI[/b] library, and tModLoader grabs it for you automatically. Transformation is synced in multiplayer, and all the puppy extras live in [b]Configs[/b]: dog skin, bark volume, pitch and more. If something's off, reload mods and give us a woof.
