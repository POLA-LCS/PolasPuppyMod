[h1]What is Puppy Mod?[/h1]
I made Puppy Mod to add a soft, teasing [b]owner/puppy[/b] dynamic to Terraria - wholesome, affectionate, a little kinky, but [b]never explicit[/b]. Think headpats, praise, zoomies around the boss arena, and happy barks when someone says [i]"good puppy!"[/i]

I built it for couples, friends, servers, and anyone who wants to be a little more puppy while digging, fighting, and exploring. [b]Furry and therian friendly.[/b] Consensual - you choose when to put the ears on and when to take them off. Just good puppy energy.

[b]SFW and Steam-safe:[/b] Flirty and teasing, but completely wholesome.

[h1]How to Play[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]1. Become Puppy[/b] - Equip [b]Dog Ears[/b] (vanity) + [b]Dog Tail[/b]. The instant both are on you [b]bark[/b] automatically. You get both free on character creation.
[*][b]2. Choose Your Form[/b] - [b]Furry[/b] (vanity) is cozy and keeps all slots, [b]Therian[/b] (functional) is much faster and bouncier. Both give you a little extra mobility and a mining boost - now clearly shown as your armor set bonus.
[*][b]3. Get Clicked[/b] - A non-puppy Owner [b]holds[/b] The Clicker to set range, then [b]clicks[/b] to signal. Every puppy in range at that moment gets the [b]Good Puppy[/b] buff.
[*][b]4. Zoomies and Bark[/b] - Buff gives regen and zoomies, refreshed while you stay close. As a puppy you can [b]double tap UP to bark[/b] whenever you want attention.
[*][b]5. Soft Rope Leash[/b] - If a puppy wears the [b]Chain Leash[/b], any Owner can right click them bare-handed to toggle a soft [b]rope[/b] - not a rough chain, rauf! - that keeps you close and happy while tethered, with only a light tug for your Owner. Click again or unequip to release.
[/list]

[h1]The Clicker & The Leash[/h1]
Clicker is Pink rarity - hold to set praise range, click to send a crisp click. Puppies cannot use it - only an Owner can tell you you are good. The Chain Leash is the puppy's collar piece; once equipped, the rope is ready for any Owner to gently clip on. Both craft at a [b]Work Bench[/b] - check the [b]Guide[/b].

[h1]FAQ[/h1]
[b]Is this NSFW?[/b] No. 100% SFW and Steam safe.

[b]Can I play solo?[/b] Yes. Be a puppy and bark with double tap UP, or invite someone to click you or walk you.

[b]Two puppies near one Owner?[/b] Both are praised at once, leashes are per-puppy.

[b]Can puppies click?[/b] No. Puppies get clicked. Only non-puppies can use the clicker.

[b]How do I bark?[/b] Automatically when you become a puppy or get praised, plus double tap UP when you are a puppy.

[b]How does the leash work?[/b] Puppy equips Chain Leash, Owner right clicks to toggle the soft rope. While leashed you stay close and stay happy. Right click again to let go.
[/list]
