[hr][/hr]
[h1]Cute features[/h1]
[b]Become a puppy![/b] Equip any pair of [b]Dog Ears & Dog Tail[/b] (there are a variety of them) to feel instantly more puppy! You get the set free on a new character if you want it in configs. *arf!*

[b]Mix and match![/b] [b]Dog Ears[/b] helps with digging and detecting, [b]Dog Tail[/b] gives you zoomies for moving and jumping (both softer in vanity). Equip both to unlock barking skills, even in vanity!

[b]Get praised![/b] A non-puppy holding the [b]Clicker[/b] can praise every nearby puppy at once, giving them the [b]Good Puppy![/b] buff for happy zoomies! Puppies can't click, they get to be good. *clack!*

[b]Pet anyone![/b] Right-click any puppy to give them pets - they’ll wag and get hearts! You can also pet the [b]Zoologist[/b] right from her chat with [b]pet <3[/b]! Thanks to the new [b]PetAnyone[/b] utility, any NPC or puppy-set player can be made pettable. *pat pat!*

[b]Be unique![/b] Barking pitch as well as volume can be tweaked inside the client config. So go there and explore your puppy personality! *awoo!*

[hr][/hr]
[h1]become a real puppy - awoo![/h1]
With any mix of two ears and tails and [b]no mount[/b], press your [b]Quick Mount[/b] key (default [b]R[/b]) to truly transform into a dog which it's breed can be changed inside the configs!

While you're a real dog items are off and you can emote:
Playful invitation (default [b]I[/b]), bend forward and start panting and wagging.
Scratchy scratches (default [b]O[/b]), get rid of those ticks.
Random emote (default [b]P[/b])

[hr][/hr]
[h1]walkies & leashing - wraf! arf! zoooooom!!![/h1]
Wear a [b]collar[/b] and let an owner take you for walkies! With any [b]leash weapon[/b], they can right-click a collared puppy to clip on.

Once leashed links you together as you trot along, and both sides get rewarded: the [b]collar[/b] buffs the owner and the [b]leash[/b] buffs the puppy. Right-click again to unclip and run free. *wag wag!*

[hr][/hr]
[h1]good to know - woof?[/h1]
[b]Solo or with friends?[/b] Both work! Be a happy solo pupper, or bring friends to share the fun!

[b]Two puppies?[/b] Yep - the whole pack can be good together!

[b]Can puppies praise themselves?[/b] Nuh-uh - only owners praise, puppies get to be good. That's the fun! :3

[b]Technical woofs![/b] Needs the [b]TransformAPI[/b] library, and tModLoader grabs it for you automatically. Transformation is synced in multiplayer, and all the puppy extras live in [b]Configs[/b]: dog skin, bark volume, pitch and more. If something's off, reload mods and give us a woof.
