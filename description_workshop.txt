[i]Hello everypup! this is a cute, silly [b]multiplayer-friendly[/b] mod for wagging, zooming, and getting told you're a very good puppy! *pant pant*[/i]

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Getting started[/h1]
Create your character with the mod config "Start as a puppy :3" toggled or for a vanilla legit experience, buy [b]Dog Ears[/b] and [b]Dog Tail[/b] from the Zoologist.
equip them (either accessory or vanity). That, my puppy, is the beginning of an adventure of waggin, zoomin, growls and barks recorded by me so you feel like a real good puppy all the time.

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Cute features[/h1]
[b]Mix and match![/b] [b]Dog Ears[/b] helps with mining and misc, [b]Dog Tail[/b] is related to movement, zooming and jumping (both softer in vanity).

[b]Get praised![/b] A non-puppy holding the [b]Clicker[/b] can praise every nearby puppy at once, giving them the [b]Good Puppy![/b] buff for happy zoomies! Puppies can't click, they get to be good. *click! clack!*

[b]Pet ![/b] Right-click any puppy to give them pets, they’ll wag and get hearts! You can also pet the [b]Zoologist[/b] right from her chat with [b]Pet <3[/b]! Thanks to the new [b]PetAnyone[/b] utility, any NPC or puppy-set player can be made pettable. *pat pat!*

[b]Be unique![/b] Barking pitch as well as volume can be tweaked inside the client config. So go there and explore your puppy personality! *awoo!*

[hr][/hr]
[h1]Become a REAL puppy, awoo![/h1]
With any mix of two ears and tails and [b]no mount[/b], press your [b]Quick Mount[/b] key (default [b]R[/b]) to truly transform into a dog which it's breed can be changed inside the configs! Or press your dedicated [b]Dog Transformation[/b] key (default [b]T[/b]) — that one works even while a mount is equipped! *bark!*

While you're a real dog items are off and you can emote:
Playful invitation (default [b]I[/b]), bend forward and start panting and wagging.
Scratchy scratches (default [b]O[/b]), get rid of those ticks.
Random emote (default [b]P[/b])

[hr][/hr]
[h1]walkies & leashing, wraf! arf! zoooooom!!![/h1]
Wear a [b]collar[/b] and let an owner take you for walkies! With any [b]leash weapon[/b], they can right-click a collared puppy to clip on.

Once leashed links you together as you trot along, and both sides get rewarded: the [b]collar[/b] buffs the owner and the [b]leash[/b] buffs the puppy. Right-click again to unclip and run free. *wag wag!*

[hr][/hr]
[h1]good to know, woof?[/h1]
[b]Solo or with friends?[/b] Both work! Be a happy solo pupper, or bring friends to share the fun!

[b]Two puppies?[/b] Yep, the whole pack can be good together!

[b]Can puppies praise themselves?[/b] Nuh-uh, only owners praise, puppies get to be good. That's the fun! :3

[b]Technical woofs![/b] Transformation is built in and synced in multiplayer — no extra downloads. All the puppy extras live in [b]Configs[/b]: dog skin, bark volume, pitch and more. If something's off, reload mods and give us a woof.
