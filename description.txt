A puppy-focused mod for the Dog Ears + Dog Tail. Wear both for a cute + useful puppy bonus - bark (sounds recorded by me) and zoomies. Works in vanity too.
Owners can praise nearby puppies with a Clicker for the Good Puppy buff, and if you wear a collar an owner with a leash can right-click you for walkies - you both get buffed while tethered. Configs for starter gear and preferences.
